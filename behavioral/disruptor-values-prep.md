# Behavioral prep — Palo Alto Networks values (in case round 2 includes it)

PAN's culture leans on these themes (commonly cited): **Disruption, Execution,
Collaboration, Integrity, Inclusion**. Prep one tight STAR story per value. Reuse stories
from your `star-story-bank.md`; here just map them and sharpen the angle.

Keep each story to ~2 min: Situation (15s) → Task (15s) → Action (60s, "I" not "we") →
Result (30s, quantified). End with what you learned.

## Disruption (challenge status quo, bold bets)
- Prompt forms: "Tell me about a time you challenged how things were done / drove adoption
  of a new tech or pattern."
- Your story: __________
- Sharpen: what was the risk, who pushed back, what was the measurable improvement?

## Execution (ship under ambiguity/pressure)
- Prompt forms: "A time you delivered under a tight deadline / cut scope wisely."
- Your story: __________ (a time-pressured ship maps well to this 2-hr interview theme)
- Sharpen: how you prioritized, what you deliberately deferred, the outcome.

## Collaboration (cross-functional, mentorship)
- Prompt forms: "Resolved a technical disagreement / mentored someone / worked with
  backend or design."
- Your story: __________
- Sharpen: senior signal = you elevated others, not just shipped solo.

## Integrity (own mistakes, do the right thing)
- Prompt forms: "A time you made a mistake / pushed back on something wrong / gave hard
  feedback."
- Your story: __________ (your `mistake-log.md` habit is a great honest answer)
- Sharpen: what you owned, how you prevented recurrence.

## Inclusion (diverse perspectives, psychological safety)
- Prompt forms: "Made space for a quieter teammate / incorporated a dissenting view."
- Your story: __________

## Likely role-specific behavioral prompts
- "How do you use AI in your day-to-day work?" → tie to `../practice/ai-usage-playbook.md`:
  accelerator for well-defined work, you stay the engineer, you verify everything.
- "Walk me through a frontend architecture decision you're proud of." → reuse a
  `../architecture-opinions/` topic (state vs server-state, context vs external store).
- "Why Palo Alto / why security?" → genuine interest in security UX; analysts live in
  these tools; correctness and trust matter more than in most apps.
- Layoff/gap question → keep it crisp and forward-looking (see `layoff-explanation.md`).

## Reminders
- Lead with "I," quantify results, name the tradeoff.
- One honest weakness/mistake story ready — don't dodge.
- Have 2–3 sharp questions for them (team, on-call, how they measure frontend quality,
  how AI is used on the team).
